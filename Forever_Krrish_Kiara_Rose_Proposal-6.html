A letter written only for you

My Dear Kiara, ❤️

I don't know if words will ever be enough to explain what you mean to me, but I want to try anyway. Sometimes one person enters your life and slowly becomes much more than just a person. They become your favorite thought, your safest place, the smile you look for without even realizing it, and the reason an ordinary day suddenly feels special. For me, that person is you.

When I imagine my future, I don't only imagine places, success or dreams. I imagine someone standing beside me through all of it. Someone I can look at after a long day and feel at home. Someone with whom I can laugh about the stupidest things, share my biggest dreams, and sit quietly without needing to fill the silence.

And every time I imagine that person, I see you.

I want to be there for your happiest moments, but I also want to be there when life becomes difficult. I want to celebrate your victories, listen when your heart feels heavy, support the dreams you chase and remind you how special you are on the days when you forget it yourself.

I know life won't always be perfect. There will be disagreements, misunderstandings, stressful days and moments when we don't know what to say. But I don't want a love that disappears when things become difficult. I want a love that stays, talks, understands, forgives, grows and keeps finding its way back to each other.

Kiara, I don't want you to be just a chapter in my life. I want you to be part of the entire story. I want to know every little thing that makes you smile, every dream you still want to chase, every place you want to visit and every version of yourself you're going to become.

Maybe forever is a very big word. But when I imagine spending it with you, somehow it doesn't feel long enough.

If life gave me the choice to go back and choose again, knowing everything I know now, I would still choose you. Not only on the beautiful days. I would choose you on the ordinary days, the difficult days, the quiet days and every day in between.

♥ ♥ ♥
💖

Forever — Krrish & Kiara

Forever yours,
Krrish ❤️
